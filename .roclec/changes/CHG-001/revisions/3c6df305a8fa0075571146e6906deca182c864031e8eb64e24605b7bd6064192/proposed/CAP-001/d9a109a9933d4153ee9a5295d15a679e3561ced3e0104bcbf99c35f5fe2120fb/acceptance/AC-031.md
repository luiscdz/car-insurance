# AC-031: Seed content for Colombia

Requirement: REQ-007.

Given a fresh start of the project with an empty database
Then Colombia (code `co`, currency COP, locale es-CO) is published with the steps, in order: 1 "Tus datos y tu vehículo" (form), 2 "Elige tu plan" (plan list), 3 "Resumen y pago" (summary and payment)
And its form step contains, in this order and all required:
- Tipo de documento: select with "Cédula de ciudadanía" and "Cédula de extranjería";
- Número de documento: text of 6 to 10 digits;
- Fecha de nacimiento: date with a minimum age of 18 years;
- Placa: text of three letters followed by three digits (example `ABC123`);
- Marca: select with at least three vehicle makes;
- Año del vehículo: whole number from 20 years before to 1 year after the current year;
letters in these formats are accepted in upper or lower case;
each field has a Spanish label, help text and error message, and only fictitious example values are used.

**Verification:** End-to-end test against the seeded stack.
