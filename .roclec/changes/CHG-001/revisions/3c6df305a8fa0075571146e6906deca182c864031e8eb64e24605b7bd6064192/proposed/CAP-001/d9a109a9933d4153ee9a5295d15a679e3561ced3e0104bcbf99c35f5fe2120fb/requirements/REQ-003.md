# REQ-003: Field validation

Each field is validated with the validations and the single error message configured for it in Strapi, with inclusive limits and dates relative to the current date on the person's device. A form step cannot be left forward while any of its fields is invalid. The vehicle year and the policyholder's legal age are enforced through these configured validations.

Acceptance: AC-009, AC-010, AC-011, AC-012, AC-013, AC-014.
