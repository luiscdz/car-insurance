# AC-027: Programmatic labels and automated accessibility scan

Requirement: REQ-006.

Given the form steps of each country, with and without errors displayed, and with the leave dialog of AC-034 open
Then every field has a programmatic label equal to its configured label, its help text and error message are programmatically associated with it, and required fields are exposed as required
And the automated accessibility scan reports no WCAG 2.2 AA violation.

**Verification:** End-to-end test with the automated accessibility scan for Colombia and Chile, with and without errors displayed and with the leave dialog open.
