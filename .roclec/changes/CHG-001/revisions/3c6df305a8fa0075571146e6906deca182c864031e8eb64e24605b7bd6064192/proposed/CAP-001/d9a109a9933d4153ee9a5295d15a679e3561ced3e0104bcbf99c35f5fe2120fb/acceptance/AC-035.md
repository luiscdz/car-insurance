# AC-035: Going back to another step of the same wizard does not ask

Requirement: REQ-005.

Given the current step has unconfirmed values
When the person goes back to the previous step with the wizard's back action or the browser's back button, or moves to another step of the same country's wizard through the browser's history
Then no confirmation is shown
And the step they move to shows its stored values (AC-020)
And the unconfirmed values of the step they left are discarded.

**Verification:** End-to-end test for Chile (type in "Tu vehículo", go back with both mechanisms; no dialog; values of "Tu vehículo" not kept) and unit test.
