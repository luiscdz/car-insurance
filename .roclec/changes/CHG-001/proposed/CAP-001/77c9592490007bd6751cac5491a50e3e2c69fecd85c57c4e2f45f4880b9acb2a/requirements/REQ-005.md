# REQ-005: Wizard navigation and data

The person moves forward and back through the country's configured steps. The values of completed steps are kept per country for the life of the browser tab, survive going back and reloading, are reconciled with the current configuration, and never leave the browser during the form steps (P5). Direct entry to a later step leads to the first step that is not complete.

Acceptance: AC-019, AC-020, AC-021, AC-022, AC-023, AC-024, AC-025, AC-026.
