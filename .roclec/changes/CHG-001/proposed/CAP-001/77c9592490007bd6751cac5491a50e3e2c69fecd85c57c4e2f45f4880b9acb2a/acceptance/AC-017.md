# AC-017: Invalid content

Requirement: REQ-004.

Given the content received for the country fails validation, for example: a field without label or error message, an unknown field type, a select field without options, a validation with missing values, a minimum greater than its maximum or an unusable pattern, or a step sequence that breaks ADR-001 (at least one form step, then exactly one plan list, then exactly one summary and payment step as the last step)
When the wizard is loaded
Then the person sees a fallback message in Spanish stating that the form is not available right now, announced to assistive technologies, with no technical detail
And no part of the wizard is shown.

**Verification:** Unit test of the shared content schemas with valid and invalid fixtures for both countries, and unit test (component) of the message.
