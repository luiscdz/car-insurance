# AC-019: Advance to the next step

Requirement: REQ-005.

Given every field of the current form step is valid
When the person activates continue
Then the step's values are stored in the wizard data of the country
And the next step in the country's configured order is shown at its own address under the country prefix
And focus moves to that step's heading
And the progress indicator, which lists all the country's steps in configured order with their titles, marks it as the current step (with the seed: three steps for Colombia, four for Chile).

**Verification:** End-to-end test (Chile from "Tus datos" to "Tu vehículo"; Colombia leaving its form step) and unit test.
