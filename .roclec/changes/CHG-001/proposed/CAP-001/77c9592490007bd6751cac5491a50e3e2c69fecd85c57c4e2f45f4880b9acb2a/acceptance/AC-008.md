# AC-008: Wizard copy comes from Strapi

Requirement: REQ-002.

Given a country
When its wizard is shown
Then every piece of Spanish copy on the screen, other than the fallback messages of AC-002 and AC-015 to AC-018, comes from that country's content in Strapi, including step titles, field texts, the labels of the continue and back actions and the labels of the progress indicator
And changing that copy in Strapi changes the screen after a reload.

**Verification:** Unit test (component) with fixtures for both countries showing different copy.
