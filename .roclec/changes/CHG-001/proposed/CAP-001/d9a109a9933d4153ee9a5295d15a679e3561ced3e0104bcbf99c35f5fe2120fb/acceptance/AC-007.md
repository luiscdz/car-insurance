# AC-007: Published configuration change visible on reload

Requirement: REQ-002.

Given the person has opened the wizard of a country
When the business team publishes a new field in a form step of that country in Strapi
And the person reloads the application
Then the new field appears in its configured position, with no code change and no deployment
And likewise, after a reload, a field that was unpublished or deleted no longer appears, and a changed order, label, help text, error message, validation or option is shown as configured
And a field saved only as a draft never appears.

**Verification:** End-to-end test whose setup adds, reorders and unpublishes a field through Strapi with a test-only credential that never reaches the browser.
