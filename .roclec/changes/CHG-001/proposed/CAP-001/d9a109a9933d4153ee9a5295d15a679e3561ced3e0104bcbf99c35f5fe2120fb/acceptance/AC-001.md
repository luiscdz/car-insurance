# AC-001: Country selection at the root address

Requirement: REQ-001.

Given Colombia and Chile are published countries in Strapi
When the person opens the root address of the application
Then they see one choice per published country, labeled with the country name configured in Strapi
And choosing a country opens the first step of that country's wizard at `/{code}` (`/co`, `/cl`)
And a country that is not published is not offered.

The root page's heading and page title are front-end fallback copy in Spanish, because no country content exists yet at that address; apart from them, the page shows only the country names from Strapi.
When the list of countries cannot be loaded or is invalid, the states of AC-016 and AC-017 apply.

**Verification:** End-to-end test (both countries offered; choosing each opens its first step; heading and page title present) and unit test (component) of the root page copy.
