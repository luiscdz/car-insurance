# AC-013: Vehicle year limits

Requirement: REQ-003.

Given the current date on the person's device is in year Y
And the vehicle year field is configured with the range from 20 years before to 1 year after the current year (seed of AC-031 and AC-032)
When the person enters a year from Y−20 to Y+1, both included
Then the field is valid
When they enter Y−21 or earlier, Y+2 or later, or a value that is not a whole number
Then the field shows its configured error message and the step cannot advance.

Example: with Y = 2026, 2006 and 2027 are accepted; 2005 and 2028 are rejected. Colombia and Chile behave the same.

**Verification:** Unit test with a fixed current date (Y−21, Y−20, Y+1, Y+2, a decimal) and end-to-end test for Colombia and Chile (one accepted and one rejected boundary computed from the current date).
