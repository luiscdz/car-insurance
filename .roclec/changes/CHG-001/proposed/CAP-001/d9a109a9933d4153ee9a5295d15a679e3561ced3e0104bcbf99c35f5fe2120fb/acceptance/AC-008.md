# AC-008: Wizard copy comes from Strapi

Requirement: REQ-002.

Given a country
When its wizard is shown
Then every piece of Spanish copy on the screen, other than the front-end fallback copy (the messages of AC-002, AC-016 to AC-018, the loading label of AC-015 and the root page copy of AC-001), comes from that country's content in Strapi, including step titles, field texts, the labels of the continue and back actions, the labels of the progress indicator and the copy of the leave dialog of AC-034
And changing that copy in Strapi changes the screen after a reload.

The browser's own leave confirmation of AC-033 is not part of the application's copy: its text is set by the browser and cannot be customized.

**Verification:** Unit test (component) with fixtures for both countries showing different copy.
