# REQ-005: Wizard navigation and data

The person moves forward and back through the country's configured steps. The values of completed steps are kept per country for the life of the browser tab, survive going back and reloading, are reconciled with the current configuration, and never leave the browser during the form steps (P5). Direct entry to a later step leads to the first step that is not complete. Values typed in the current step and not confirmed with continue are not stored, but the person is asked before they are lost by reloading, closing the tab or leaving the wizard; going back to the previous step does not ask.

Acceptance: AC-019, AC-020, AC-021, AC-022, AC-023, AC-024, AC-025, AC-026, AC-033, AC-034, AC-035.
