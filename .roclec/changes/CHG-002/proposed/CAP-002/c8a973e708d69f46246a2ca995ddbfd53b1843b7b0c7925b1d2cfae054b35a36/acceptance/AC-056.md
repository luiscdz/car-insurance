# AC-056: Direct entry to the plan step and to the summary step

Requirement: REQ-010.

Given a form step of the country has not been completed with valid data in this tab
When the person opens the address of the plan step or of the summary step directly (typed, bookmarked or shared)
Then they are taken to the first step that is not complete, with no error displayed (AC-024).

Given every form step is complete but there is no chosen plan with a final price obtained for the current data
When the person opens the address of the summary step directly
Then they are taken to the plan step, with no error displayed.

The plan step is complete only while a plan is chosen and its final price for the current step 1 data is stored.

**Verification:** End-to-end test for one country (plan step address with an empty tab; summary step address with complete form steps and no chosen plan); unit test of the step resolution.
