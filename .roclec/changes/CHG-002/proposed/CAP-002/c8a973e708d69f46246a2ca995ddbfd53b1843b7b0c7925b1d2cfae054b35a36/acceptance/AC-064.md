1. "Plan Básico", base price 1200000, not recommended, with no benefits;
2. "Plan Clásico", base price 1850000, recommended, with at least one benefit;

And in Colombia's form step, "Fecha de nacimiento" is marked as the birth date for pricing and "Año del vehículo" as the vehicle year for pricing (AC-044)
And only fictitious values are used.
