# AC-049: Quick or repeated choices

Requirement: REQ-010.

Given the person is on the plan step
When they choose several plans in quick succession
Then only the final price of the plan chosen last is shown and stored, and answers to requests for plans chosen earlier are ignored, whatever order they arrive in
When they choose the plan that is already chosen
Then no new final price request is sent and nothing on the screen changes.

**Verification:** Unit test (component) with delayed answers arriving out of order.
