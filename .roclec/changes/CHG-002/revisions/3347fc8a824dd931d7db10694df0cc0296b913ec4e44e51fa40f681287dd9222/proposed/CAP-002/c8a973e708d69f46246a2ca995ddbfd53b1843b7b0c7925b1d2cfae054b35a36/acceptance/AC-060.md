# AC-060: Accessible plan list and automated accessibility scan

Requirement: REQ-012.

Given the plan step of each country, with no plan chosen, with a plan chosen and its final price shown, with the message asking to choose a plan, with the price error of AC-047, and with the no-plans message of AC-058
Then the plans are exposed as one group whose programmatic name is the step heading
And each plan's choice control has an accessible name that includes the plan's name, and its chosen state is exposed
And the recommended badge text, the base price and, when shown, the final price of each plan can be perceived by assistive technologies together with that plan
And the waiting text, the final price, the error messages and the no-plans message are announced as they appear
And the automated accessibility scan reports no WCAG 2.2 AA violation.

**Verification:** End-to-end test with the automated accessibility scan for Colombia and Chile in each state above; unit test (component) of the accessible names and announcements.
