# AC-039: Plan configuration change without deployment

Requirement: REQ-008.

Given the person has opened the wizard of a country
When the business team, in Strapi, publishes a new active plan for that country, deactivates or unpublishes a plan, or changes a plan's order, base price, coverages, benefits or recommended flag
And the person reloads the application
Then the plan step shows the plans as now configured, with no code change and no deployment
And a plan saved only as a draft, or published but not active, never appears.

**Verification:** End-to-end test whose setup adds, reorders and deactivates a plan through Strapi with a test-only credential that never reaches the browser, and restores the seed afterwards.
