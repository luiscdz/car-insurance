# AC-045: Final price of the chosen plan

Requirement: REQ-009.

Given the plan step shows the country's plans, each with its base price
When the person chooses a plan
Then the final price of that plan is requested from the price-quote endpoint with the inputs of AC-044
And while it is being obtained, the chosen plan shows, in place of its final price, the text configured for that wait, announced to assistive technologies as a status, and no other plan shows a final price
When the final price arrives
Then the chosen plan shows it, formatted as in AC-042 and next to the final price label, in addition to its base price, and the final price is announced as a status.

The endpoint computes the final price with the rule of AC-043 from the plan's base price as published and active in Strapi for that country at the time of the request, never from a price sent by the browser. A plan that does not exist, is not published, is not active or belongs to another country, and inputs that are not whole numbers, get an error answer (AC-047).

**Verification:** Unit test (component) of the waiting and final price states; unit test of the price-quote endpoint (published active plan; unpublished, inactive and other-country plan; malformed inputs); end-to-end test for Colombia and Chile whose step 1 data gives a known adjustment, checking the final price shown.
