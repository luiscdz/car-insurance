# AC-050: Move on to the summary

Requirement: REQ-010.

Given a plan is chosen and its final price is shown
When the person activates continue (label from Strapi, AC-008)
Then the chosen plan and its final price are kept in the country's wizard data
And the next step in the country's configured order, the summary and payment step, is shown at its own address under the country prefix (with the seed: `/co/resumen-y-pago` and `/cl/resumen-y-pago`)
And focus moves to that step's heading, and the progress indicator marks it as the current step and the plan step as complete, as in AC-019.

The plan step also offers the back action to the previous step (AC-020). What the summary and payment step shows is specified by user story US-03 and is outside this change.

**Verification:** End-to-end test for Colombia and Chile from the plan step to the summary step's address, heading and progress; unit test (component).
