# AC-054: Changing step 1 data after choosing a plan

Requirement: REQ-010.

Given a plan is chosen and its final price was obtained
When the person goes back and confirms a form step with continue with values that differ from the values stored for that step
Then the chosen plan is kept, the stored final price is discarded, and the plan step is not complete until a new final price is obtained
And opening the address of the summary step leads to the plan step (AC-056)
And when the plan step is shown, the final price is requested with the inputs derived from the new values (AC-044, AC-045).

Confirming a form step with unchanged values keeps the stored final price.

**Verification:** Unit test of the wizard data store; end-to-end test for one country changing the birth date to one in another age bracket and checking the new final price.
