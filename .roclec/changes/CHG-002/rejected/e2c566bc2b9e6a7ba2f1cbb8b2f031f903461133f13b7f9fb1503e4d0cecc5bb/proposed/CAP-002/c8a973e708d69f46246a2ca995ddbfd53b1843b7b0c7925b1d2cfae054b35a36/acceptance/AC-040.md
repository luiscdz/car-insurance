# AC-040: Plan list copy comes from Strapi

Requirement: REQ-008.

Given a country
When its plan step is shown
Then every piece of Spanish copy on the step comes from that country's content in Strapi: the step title (AC-008), the labels of the continue and back actions (AC-008), and the plan list copy, namely the label of the action that chooses a plan, the text that marks the chosen plan, the recommended badge text, the base price label, the final price label, the text shown while the final price is obtained, the coverages heading, the benefits heading, the no-plans message, the message asking to choose a plan, the price error message and the label of its retry action
And changing that copy in Strapi changes the screen after a reload.

The only exceptions are the front-end fallback states of AC-059 (those of AC-015 to AC-017).
Missing or blank plan list copy makes the country's content invalid (AC-017).

**Verification:** Unit test (component) with fixtures for both countries showing different copy; unit test of the shared content schemas with copy missing.
