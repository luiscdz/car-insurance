# AC-057: No leave confirmation on the plan step

Requirement: REQ-010.

Given the person is on the plan step, with or without a chosen plan
When they reload the page, close the tab, go back to a form step, or navigate within the application to the root address or to another country's wizard
Then neither the browser's leave confirmation of AC-033 nor the application's dialog of AC-034 is shown on account of the plan step
Because the choice is stored when it is made (AC-048), the plan step never has unconfirmed values, and the choice is kept after any of these actions (AC-053).

**Verification:** Unit test (the plan step reports no unconfirmed values); end-to-end test for one country reloading the plan step with a chosen plan and going back from it, with no confirmation shown.
