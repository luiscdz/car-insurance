# AC-053: The choice is kept when going back, forward or reloading

Requirement: REQ-010.

Given the person chose a plan in the country's wizard in this tab
When they return to the plan step after going back to a form step (with the back action or the browser's back button) and coming forward again (with continue or the browser's forward button), or after reloading the page on the plan step or on the summary step
Then the plan step shows the same plan as chosen
And the final price of that plan is requested again as in AC-045: the final price is requested every time the plan step is shown with a chosen plan
And the choice is kept per country and per tab like the rest of the wizard data (AC-023): it does not appear in the other country's wizard and it is gone in a new tab.

**Verification:** End-to-end test for one country (back and forward with both mechanisms, reload on the plan step and on the summary step) and for the per-country separation; unit test of the wizard data store.
