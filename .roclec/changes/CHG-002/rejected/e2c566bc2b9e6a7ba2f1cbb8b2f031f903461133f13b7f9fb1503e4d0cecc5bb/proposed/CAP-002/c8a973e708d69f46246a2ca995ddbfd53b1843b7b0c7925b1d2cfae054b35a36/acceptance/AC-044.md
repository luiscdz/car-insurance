# AC-044: Inputs of the price rule

Requirement: REQ-009.

Given the country's form fields in Strapi
Then a field can be marked as the policyholder's birth date for pricing, or as the vehicle year for pricing, and no other pricing input exists
And in each country at most one field carries each mark; the birth date mark is allowed only on a date field and the vehicle year mark only on a number field
And a mark on a field of another type, or the same mark on two fields of the country, makes the country's content invalid (AC-017).

Given the stored values of the country's completed form steps
When the inputs of the rule are derived on the person's device
Then the policyholder's age A is the number of whole years completed on the device's current date since the value of the field marked as birth date, computed as for the legal age of AC-014 (a 29 February birthday is reached on 1 March in years that are not leap years)
And the vehicle's age V is the device's current year minus the value of the field marked as vehicle year
And when no field carries a mark, or the marked field has no stored value, that input is not available.

The marks are configuration: no code names a field, a country or a step to find the inputs.

**Verification:** Unit test of the input derivation with a fixed date and fixtures for both countries (Colombia with both marks in one form step, Chile with the marks in different form steps, a country with no marks); unit test of the shared content schemas with a duplicated mark and a mark on a field of the wrong type.
