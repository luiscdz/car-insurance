# REQ-014: Summary of the quote

The summary and payment step of the country's wizard shows the data the person confirmed in every form step, grouped by form step in configured order, each value presented in readable form and the fields marked in Strapi as masked shown partially masked, together with the chosen plan and the final price obtained on the plan step. From the summary the person can return to any form step to edit their data and to the plan step to change the plan. The summary copy comes from the country's content in Strapi and is validated before use (P2, P3). No behavior depends on code that names a specific country, step or field (P1, ADR-001).

Acceptance: AC-067, AC-068, AC-069, AC-070, AC-071, AC-072, AC-073.
