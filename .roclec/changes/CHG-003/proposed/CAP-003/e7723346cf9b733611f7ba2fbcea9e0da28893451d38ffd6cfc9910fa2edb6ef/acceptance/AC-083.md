# AC-083: Confirmation after an approved payment

Requirement: REQ-017.

Given the person paid with the terms accepted
When the endpoint approves the payment
Then, in place of the summary and at the summary step's address, the person sees the confirmation title and the confirmation message from Strapi, the quote number next to its label, the plan's name next to the plan label, and the amount paid, which is the final price shown on the summary formatted as in AC-042, next to its label
And focus moves to the confirmation title, and the confirmation is announced
And the progress indicator marks every step of the country as complete
And neither the pay action, the terms, the edit actions, the change plan action nor the back action is shown; the new quote action (AC-087) is
And no value entered in a form step, masked or not, is shown on the confirmation.

**Verification:** Unit test (component) with fixtures for both countries; end-to-end test for Colombia and Chile covering the complete flow from country selection to the confirmation.
