# AC-086: Changing the quote after the confirmation

Requirement: REQ-017.

Given a confirmation is stored for the country in this tab
When the person goes back to an earlier step
Then that step shows its stored data as usual (AC-020, AC-053)
When the person confirms a form step with values different from the stored ones, or chooses a plan other than the paid one, or the wizard loads and reconciliation discards the chosen plan or changes stored values (AC-025, AC-055)
Then the confirmation is discarded, and the next time the summary step is shown it is the summary of the changed quote, with the terms unaccepted and the pay action, as a new quote
When the person only confirms unchanged values, chooses the paid plan again, or the plan step obtains the final price again
Then the confirmation stays.

**Verification:** Unit test of the wizard data store (each action that discards and each that keeps the confirmation); end-to-end test for Chile (after the confirmation, browser back to the plan step, choose another plan, continue: the summary with the pay action is shown).
