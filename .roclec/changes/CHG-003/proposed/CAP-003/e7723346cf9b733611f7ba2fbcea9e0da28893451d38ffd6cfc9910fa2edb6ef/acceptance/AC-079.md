# AC-079: Only the country and the plan leave the browser to pay

Requirement: REQ-016.

Given the person pays
Then the payment request carries only the country code and the chosen plan's identifier
And no value entered in a form step, such as the document number, the RUT, the birth date, the license plate, the make or the vehicle year, appears in the payment request, in any other request sent to Strapi, in the address or in the browser console, on the summary step or on the confirmation
And the payment endpoint stores nothing (no payment, no quote, no quote number, no personal data) and does not log the inputs it receives.

This criterion keeps AC-026 and AC-046 true on the summary step and the confirmation.

**Verification:** End-to-end test observing the requests, the address and the console on the summary and the confirmation for Colombia and Chile; unit test of the payment endpoint showing that it writes nothing and logs no input.
