# AC-076: Accepting the terms enables pay

Requirement: REQ-015.

Given the summary is shown
Then the terms acceptance checkbox, whose label is the terms acceptance label from Strapi, is not checked
And the pay action is shown with the disabled style and exposed as disabled to assistive technologies, but stays focusable (as AC-011 and AC-051)
When the person activates pay without having accepted the terms
Then no payment request is sent, the message asking to accept the terms, from Strapi, is shown next to the checkbox, programmatically associated with it and announced, and focus moves to the checkbox
When the person checks the checkbox
Then the message disappears and the pay action is enabled
When the person unchecks it
Then the pay action is disabled again as above.

Accepting does not require opening the terms. The acceptance is not stored: it starts unchecked every time the summary step is shown (AC-089).

**Verification:** Unit test (component) of every transition; end-to-end test for Colombia and Chile (activating pay without accepting sends no request and shows the message; accepting enables pay).
