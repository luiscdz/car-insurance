# REQ-015: Terms and conditions per country

Each country has one published terms and conditions text in Strapi, related to the country (ADR-001). The summary lets the person read it and requires accepting it before paying; the acceptance is not stored. Terms are requested with the rest of the country's content when the wizard loads and are validated before use: missing, duplicated or blank terms are invalid content (P3). A change published in Strapi is visible on the next load without a code change or a deployment (P2).

Acceptance: AC-074, AC-075, AC-076, AC-077.
