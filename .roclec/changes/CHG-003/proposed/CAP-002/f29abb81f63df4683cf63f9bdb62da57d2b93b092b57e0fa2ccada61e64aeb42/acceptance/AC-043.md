# AC-043: Documented simulated price rule

Requirement: REQ-009.

This criterion documents the simulated price rule. It is a simulation (P5), not a real rating, and it is the same for every country: no value of the rule comes from or differs by country.

Given a plan with base price B (a whole amount of the country's currency), the policyholder's age A and the vehicle's age V, both in whole years (AC-044)
When the final price is computed
Then it is B × (1 + vehicle adjustment + age adjustment), computed exactly and rounded to the nearest whole amount, with halves rounded up, where:
- vehicle adjustment is 0 when V ≤ 5, 0.10 when 6 ≤ V ≤ 10 and 0.20 when V ≥ 11 (a vehicle of next year's model has V = −1);
- age adjustment is 0.15 when A ≤ 25, 0 when 26 ≤ A ≤ 64 and 0.10 when A ≥ 65;
- an input that is not available (AC-044) contributes 0.

Examples, with fictitious values:

| B | A | V | Final price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1000000 | 40 | 3 | 1000000 |
| 1000000 | 22 | 8 | 1250000 |
| 1000000 | 70 | 15 | 1300000 |
| 350000 | 25 | 6 | 437500 |
| 350000 | 26 | 5 | 350000 |
| 333333 | 30 | 10 | 366666 |
| 12345 | 30 | 10 | 13580 |
| 520000 | not available | 11 | 624000 |

**Verification:** Unit test of the rule as a pure function, with the examples above and every bracket boundary (V = 5, 6, 10, 11; A = 25, 26, 64, 65), V = −1 and unavailable inputs.
