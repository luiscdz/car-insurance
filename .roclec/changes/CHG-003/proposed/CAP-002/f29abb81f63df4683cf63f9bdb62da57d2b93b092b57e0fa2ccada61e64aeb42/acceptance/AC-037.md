# AC-037: Content of each plan

Requirement: REQ-008.

Given a valid plan of the country (AC-041)
When the plan list is shown
Then the plan shows, as configured in Strapi: its name, its short description, its base price formatted as in AC-042 next to the base price label, its coverages in the order they are configured, and an action to choose it
And each coverage shows its name and, when configured, its highlighted value (free text, for example a limit), visually emphasized
And the plan's benefits are listed under the benefits heading in the order they are configured; when the plan has no benefits, the benefits heading and list are not shown.

**Verification:** Unit test (component) with fixtures for both countries, including a plan without benefits and a coverage without highlighted value; end-to-end test checking one seeded plan of each country.
