# AC-072: Change the plan from the summary

Requirement: REQ-014.

Given the summary is shown
When the person activates the change plan action (label from the summary copy), the wizard's back action or the browser's back button
Then the plan step is shown at its own address with the chosen plan marked (AC-053), and focus moves to its heading
When the person chooses another plan and activates continue once its final price is shown (AC-050)
Then the summary shows the newly chosen plan and its final price.

**Verification:** End-to-end test for Colombia (change plan action, choose another plan, continue) and for Chile (browser back from the summary); unit test (component).
