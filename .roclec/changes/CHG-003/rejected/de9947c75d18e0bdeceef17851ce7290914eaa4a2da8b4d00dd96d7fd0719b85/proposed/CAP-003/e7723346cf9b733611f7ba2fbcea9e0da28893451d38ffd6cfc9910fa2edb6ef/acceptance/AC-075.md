# AC-075: Terms dialog behavior

Requirement: REQ-015.

Given the person activated the view terms action
Then the terms dialog is modal, its programmatic name is its title, and focus stays inside it until it closes
And when it opens, focus moves to its title, so reading starts at the top of the text
And a text longer than the visible area scrolls inside the dialog, while the title and the close action stay reachable
When the person activates the close action or presses Escape
Then the dialog closes, focus returns to the view terms action, and the terms acceptance is unchanged.

Opening the dialog is not required to accept the terms (AC-076).

**Verification:** Unit test (component) of focus on open, Escape, close action and focus return; end-to-end test for one country (open, close with Escape, open, close with the close action).
