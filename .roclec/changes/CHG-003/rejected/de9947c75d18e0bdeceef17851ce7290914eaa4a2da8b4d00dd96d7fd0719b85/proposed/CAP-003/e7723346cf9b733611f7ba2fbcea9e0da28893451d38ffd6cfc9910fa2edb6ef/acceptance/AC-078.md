# AC-078: Pay sends one request and locks

Requirement: REQ-016.

Given the terms are accepted
When the person activates pay
Then exactly one payment request is sent to the simulated payment endpoint
And until its answer arrives, the pay action shows the processing text from Strapi, is exposed as disabled and busy, and the processing text is announced as a status
And activating pay again, by double click, repeated key presses or any other means, sends no other request
And the terms checkbox, the view terms action, the edit actions, the change plan action and the back action cannot be operated (disabled style, exposed as disabled).

**Verification:** Unit test (component) of the locked state while the answer is pending; end-to-end test for Colombia and Chile counting the payment requests after a double click on pay with a delayed answer.
