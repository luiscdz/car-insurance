# AC-067: Entered data grouped by form step

Requirement: REQ-014.

Given every form step of the country is complete and a plan is chosen with its final price stored (AC-056)
When the summary and payment step is shown
Then, for each form step of the country in configured order, the summary shows a section headed by that step's title from Strapi
And each section lists every field of that step that has a stored value, in configured order, as the field's label from Strapi and its value presented as in AC-068, or masked as in AC-069
And with the seed, Colombia shows one section, "Tus datos y tu vehículo", with its six fields, and Chile shows two sections, "Tus datos" (RUT, Fecha de nacimiento) and "Tu vehículo" (Patente, Marca, Año del vehículo).

Only data stored for that country in this tab is shown. No code names a country, a step or a field to build the summary.

**Verification:** Unit test (component) with fixtures for both countries (one form step and two form steps); end-to-end test for Colombia and Chile from the form steps to the summary, checking every section title, label and value.
