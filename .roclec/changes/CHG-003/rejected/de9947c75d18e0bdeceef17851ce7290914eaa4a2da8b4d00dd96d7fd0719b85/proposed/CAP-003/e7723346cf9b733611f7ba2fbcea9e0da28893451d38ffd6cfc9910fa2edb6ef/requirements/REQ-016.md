# REQ-016: Simulated payment

After accepting the terms, the person pays through the stateless simulated payment endpoint; there is no real gateway and no card data (P5). One activation sends exactly one payment request, and the pay action stays locked while it is processed. Only the country and the chosen plan leave the browser; the endpoint stores and logs nothing, and the browser uses no token (P4). A rejected or failed payment shows a clear message and lets the person try again with all their information kept.

Acceptance: AC-078, AC-079, AC-080, AC-081, AC-082.
