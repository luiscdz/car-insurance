# AC-073: Summary copy comes from Strapi

Requirement: REQ-014.

Given a country
When its summary step or its confirmation is shown
Then every piece of Spanish copy on them comes from that country's content in Strapi: the step title and the back label (AC-008), the step titles heading the data sections (AC-067), the field labels and option labels (AC-068), and the summary copy, namely the plan section heading, the final price label, the edit action label, the change plan label, the yes and no texts for checkbox values, the terms acceptance label, the view terms action label, the terms dialog title and its close label, the message asking to accept the terms, the pay label, the text shown while the payment is processed, the payment error message, the confirmation title, the confirmation message, the quote number label, the plan label and the amount paid label of the confirmation, and the new quote label
And changing that copy in Strapi changes the screen after a reload.

The only exceptions are the front-end fallback states of AC-015 to AC-017 (AC-077).
Missing or blank summary copy makes the country's content invalid (AC-017).

**Verification:** Unit test (component) with fixtures for both countries showing different copy; unit test of the shared content schemas with summary copy missing or blank.
