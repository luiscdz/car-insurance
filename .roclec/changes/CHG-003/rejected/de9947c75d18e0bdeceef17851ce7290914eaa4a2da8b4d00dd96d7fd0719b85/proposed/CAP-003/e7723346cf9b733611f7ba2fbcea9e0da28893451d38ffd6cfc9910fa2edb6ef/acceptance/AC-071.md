# AC-071: Edit data from the summary

Requirement: REQ-014.

Given the summary is shown
Then each data section has an edit action whose label comes from the summary copy and whose accessible name also includes the section's title
When the person activates the edit action of a section
Then that form step is shown at its own address with its stored values in full and no error displayed (as AC-020), focus moves to its heading, and the progress indicator marks it as the current step
And from there the person moves on through the following steps in configured order with continue:
confirming unchanged values keeps the chosen plan, and the plan step requests its final price again when shown (AC-053);
confirming changed values keeps the chosen plan and requires a new final price (AC-054);
and the plan step's continue leads to the summary again (AC-050).

There is no shortcut from an edited form step back to the summary. Values typed and not confirmed follow AC-033 to AC-035.

**Verification:** End-to-end test for Chile (edit "Tu vehículo" from the summary, change the vehicle year, continue through the plan step and see the new final price on the summary) and for Colombia (edit, continue with unchanged values, return to the summary); unit test (component) of the edit actions and their accessible names.
