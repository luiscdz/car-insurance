# AC-074: Terms text of the country

Requirement: REQ-015.

Given a country with its terms and conditions published in Strapi
When the person on the summary activates the view terms action, placed below the pay action
Then a dialog shows the terms dialog title from the summary copy and the complete text of that country's published terms, exactly as configured: blocks separated by a blank line are shown as separate paragraphs, line breaks inside a block are kept, and no markup is interpreted (text such as `<b>` or `**` is shown literally)
And Colombia and Chile each show their own, different text
And a change to the terms published in Strapi is shown after a reload, and a draft is never shown.

**Verification:** End-to-end test for Colombia and Chile comparing the dialog text with the seeded terms (AC-094, AC-095), and for one country with a terms change published and then restored through the Strapi admin API (as AC-007); unit test (component) of the paragraph rendering with markup-like text.
