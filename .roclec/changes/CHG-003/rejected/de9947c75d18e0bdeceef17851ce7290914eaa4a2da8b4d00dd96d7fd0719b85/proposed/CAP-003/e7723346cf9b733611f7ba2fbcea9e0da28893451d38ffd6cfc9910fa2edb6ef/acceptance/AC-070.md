# AC-070: Chosen plan and its final price

Requirement: REQ-014.

Given a plan is chosen and its final price for the current data is stored (AC-056)
When the summary is shown
Then a section headed by the plan section heading from Strapi shows the chosen plan's name and, next to the final price label of the summary copy, its final price formatted as in AC-042
And that final price is the one obtained on the plan step and stored with the choice (AC-045, AC-050); the summary does not request it again
And no other plan, no base price, no coverage and no benefit is shown on the summary.

**Verification:** Unit test (component) with fixtures for both countries; end-to-end test for Colombia and Chile checking that the summary shows the plan name and the final price that the plan step showed.
