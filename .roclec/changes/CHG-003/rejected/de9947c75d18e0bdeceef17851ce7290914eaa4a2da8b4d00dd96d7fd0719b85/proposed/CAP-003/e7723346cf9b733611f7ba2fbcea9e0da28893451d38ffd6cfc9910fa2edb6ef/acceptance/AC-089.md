# AC-089: Reloading or leaving the summary step

Requirement: REQ-018.

Given the summary is shown, with or without the terms accepted
When the person reloads the page, closes the tab, goes back, or navigates within the application to the root address or to another country's wizard
Then neither the browser's leave confirmation of AC-033 nor the application's dialog of AC-034 is shown on account of the summary step, because it has no field values to confirm
And after a reload the summary is shown again with the same data, plan and final price and with the terms unaccepted (AC-076).

Given a payment request is being processed
When the person reloads, closes the tab or leaves the summary step
Then no confirmation is asked, the answer is ignored when it arrives, no confirmation is stored, and on return the summary is shown again unpaid; the payment is simulated and stateless, so nothing was charged or recorded.

**Verification:** Unit test (the summary step reports no unconfirmed values; an answer that arrives after leaving is ignored); end-to-end test for one country (reload with the terms accepted shows no confirmation and the terms unaccepted).
