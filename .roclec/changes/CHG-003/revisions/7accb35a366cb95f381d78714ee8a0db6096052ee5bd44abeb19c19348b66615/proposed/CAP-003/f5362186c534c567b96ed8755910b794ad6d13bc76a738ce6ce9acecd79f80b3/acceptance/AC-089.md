# AC-089: Reloading or leaving the summary step when no payment is processed

Requirement: REQ-018.

Given the summary is shown, with or without the terms accepted, and no payment request is being processed (before paying, or after a rejected or failed payment)
When the person reloads the page, closes the tab, goes back, or navigates within the application to another step, to the root address or to another country's wizard
Then neither the browser's leave confirmation of AC-033 nor the application's dialog of AC-034 is shown on account of the summary step, because it has no field values to confirm
And after a reload the summary is shown again with the same data, plan and final price and with the terms unaccepted (AC-076).

The confirmation of AC-083 never asks before leaving either.

**Verification:** Unit test (the summary step reports nothing to lose when no payment is processed); end-to-end test for one country (reload with the terms accepted shows no confirmation and the terms unaccepted; going back shows no dialog).
