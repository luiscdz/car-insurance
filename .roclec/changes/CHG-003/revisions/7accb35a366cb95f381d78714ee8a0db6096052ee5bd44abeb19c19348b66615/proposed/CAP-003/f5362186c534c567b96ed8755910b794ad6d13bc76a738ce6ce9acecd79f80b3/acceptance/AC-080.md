# AC-080: Outcome of the simulated payment

Requirement: REQ-016.

Given a payment request for a country code and a plan identifier
When that plan exists, is published and active, and belongs to that country in Strapi at the time of the request
Then the endpoint approves the payment and answers with a new simulated quote number (AC-084)
When the plan does not exist, is not published, is not active or belongs to another country, or the request is malformed
Then the endpoint answers that the payment is rejected, with no quote number and no technical detail.

No other condition rejects a payment: the outcome never depends on chance, on the time or on personal data. With the seed, a payment for an offered plan is approved; a rejection happens when the chosen plan is deactivated or unpublished after the summary was shown, and tests also produce it by answering the request in the browser (AC-081).

**Verification:** Unit test of the payment endpoint (published active plan of the country; unknown, unpublished, inactive and other-country plan; malformed request).
