# AC-051: Continue without a chosen plan or without a final price

Requirement: REQ-010.

Given no plan is chosen
Then continue is shown with the disabled style and exposed as disabled to assistive technologies, but stays focusable (as AC-011)
When the person activates it
Then the message asking to choose a plan, from Strapi, is shown next to the plans, programmatically associated with the group of plans and announced
And focus moves to the choice control of the first plan, and the step does not advance
When the person then chooses a plan
Then the message disappears.

Given a plan is chosen but its final price is still being obtained or could not be obtained (AC-045, AC-047)
When the person activates continue
Then the step does not advance, and focus moves to the chosen plan's price status, or to its retry action when the price could not be obtained.

**Verification:** Unit test (component) of both cases; end-to-end test for one country activating continue with no plan chosen.
