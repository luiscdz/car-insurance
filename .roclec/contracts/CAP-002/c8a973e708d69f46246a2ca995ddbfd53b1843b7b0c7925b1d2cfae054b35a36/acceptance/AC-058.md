# AC-058: No plans available

Requirement: REQ-011.

Given the country has no plan that is both published and active in Strapi (none exist, or they are all inactive or saved only as drafts)
When the person who completed the form steps reaches the plan step
Then the no-plans message from Strapi is shown in place of the plans, announced to assistive technologies
And no plan, no choice control and no continue action is shown, while the back action is shown
And the summary step cannot be reached (AC-056).

The plans of the other country are never offered instead.

**Verification:** Unit test (component); end-to-end test for one country whose plans are deactivated by the test setup with a test-only credential that never reaches the browser, and restored afterwards.
