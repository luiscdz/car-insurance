# Test Objective

> Source: *Technical assessment - Frontend.pdf* — Front-End Technical Test – Multi-Country Car Insurance

This test measures your ability to build software with an **AI-driven development flow**, not just your ability to code. You will build a multi-country car insurance quoting app with Angular and Strapi, and what matters most is how you design, operate and control the AI system that helps you do it.

There is no single "correct" flow we expect. You decide which agents, subagents and skills to use, in what order, and at which points a person steps in. We evaluate whether your decisions are:

- **Explicit**
- **Justified**
- **Traceable**
- **Repeatable**

Using AI tools is **allowed and required**.
